WHY COPPER, NOT RED AND GREEN
Red and green mean program and preview. They belong to the operator-facing app, and a library that never renders a bus should not borrow them. Copper is the wire. Ink, greys, radii and both typefaces are shared with webATEM, so the two still sit together.
Copper on ink is 7.4:1, so it is safe for link and heading text as well as fills. On copper fills use ink #0E0F10, never white.
